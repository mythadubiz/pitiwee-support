❓ FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the app → tap the ⚙️ gear icon → tap "Unlock All" → tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom.


How do I change the language?
Tap the ⚙️ gear icon from the library → choose from 9 available languages. The narration and text switch instantly.


The narration is not playing. What should I do?
Make sure your device is not in silent mode. You can also toggle narration on/off using the 📖 icon while reading.


Can I record my own voice to narrate the story?
Yes! While reading, tap the 🎙 icon on any page to record your own narration. Enable "Use my recordings" in Settings to play your voice instead of the built-in narration.


How many episodes are included for free?
Episode 1 is completely free. Episodes 2 through 7 require a one-time premium purchase.


Is my data collected?
No. Pitiwee does not collect any personal data. Voice recordings are stored only on your device. Read our Privacy Policy.
